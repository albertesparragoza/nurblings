The following are not covered by the MIT license and remain reserved by

- the names **Nurbi** and **Nurblings**, used as the name of
  a product, character, library or service
- the flagship character Nurbi and its artwork, which `nurbi()` in
  `nurblings/nurbi` returns

Every avatar the library generates is public domain under CC0. Nurbi's exact
body design and colours are not among the generator's choices; only `nurbi()`
returns his artwork.

What is fine without asking:

- using the library and the avatars it generates, including in commercial work
- saying your project "uses Nurblings" or "is built with Nurblings"
- forking the code, as long as a fork that ships under its own name does not
  call itself Nurblings or present Nurbi as its character

What needs permission:

- publishing a fork, package or service under the Nurblings or Nurbi name
- using the flagship character as a mascot, logo or endorsement

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask.
