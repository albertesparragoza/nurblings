Shared-element transitions: tag an avatar with `transition: key`, then wrap a change in `morph()` from `nurblings/transition`, and a Nurbling glides from a dialog into a list, or a hero into a header. Native View Transitions where each key is on screen once, a transform-only FLIP animation otherwise, nothing under reduced motion. A separate entry of about 1 KB.
