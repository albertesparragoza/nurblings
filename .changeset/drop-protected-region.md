The generator no longer redraws seeds that land near Nurbi's look: Nurbi is his own entry now, and his exact design and colours were never among the choices. About 0.7% of generation 1 avatars change with the built-in colours, and up to 14% with the themes whose colours sit near his (`punch`, `sorbet`, `lagoon`). The core is about 180 B smaller compressed.
