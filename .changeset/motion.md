Nurblings are alive by default: the body breathes, the eyes blink, the antennae drift and wiggle on hover. CSS only, still id-free, never under reduced motion or at 32 px and below. `animate: false` draws a still avatar; `animate: { breath, blink, antennae, hover, speed }` picks layers. The drawing itself is unchanged.
